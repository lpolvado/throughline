[ The Idea ]

§ 02

Most consultants arrive with frameworks. We skip the jargon and get to the answer. Then we help you ship it.

We start by living inside your customer's workflow. The constraints, the friction, the moment they swear at the screen. That's where the real product roadmap is hiding, not in a competitive analysis grid.

Then we translate. From messy reality into a prioritized list of things to ship, say, and stop doing. We're cool to work with. We don't charge by the hour. And we don't end every sentence with "circle back."

[ Process ]

§ 04

Three steps.
No theater.

01 Listen

Get inside the workflow

We talk to your customers, your team, and your data. Two weeks, no slides. Just notes, transcripts, and the things nobody's saying out loud.

02 Translate

Turn it into a plan

A short, sharp document. What to do, why, in what order, and what to stop doing. Designed to be acted on by Friday, not parsed by a steering committee.

03 Ship

Build with you, not at you

We embed with your team and execute. Positioning written. Roadmap shipped. Brand launched. We leave when it's done, or stay on retainer if it's working.
