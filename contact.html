[ FAQ ]

§ Common questions

Things people always ask.

How long until you can start? +

Usually 2–4 weeks from signed contract. Sometimes faster for sprints. We don't take on more than we can do well, so occasionally we'll suggest a start date a month out, or refer you to someone in our network if you can't wait.

What does it cost? +

Sprints start in the low five figures. Full projects sit in the mid-five-figure range. Fractional retainers are monthly. Every quote is fixed-price and tied to specific deliverables. No hourly meters running. We'll give you a real number after the scoping call.

Do you work with early-stage startups? +

Yes, but only if there's a real budget and a real problem. We're not a discount option for pre-seed founders, but we love working with seed-to-Series-A teams who've raised, hired, and now need help making smart bets.

Can you do execution, not just strategy? +

That's the whole point. We embed with your team and help ship the work. For specialty execution like design, dev, video, or paid media, we bring in vetted partners, coordinated by us, contracted with you.

Are you a solo consultant or a firm? +

A small senior team plus a network of trusted specialists we bring in by project. We stay small on purpose. It keeps the people in the room the people doing the work.

What if we're not ready yet? +

Send a note anyway. We'll tell you honestly if you should wait, what to do in the meantime, and we'll remember you when the timing's right. No CRM nurture sequence required.
