[ About / 01 ]

We exist because most consultancies don't actually ship.

[ Origin ]

§ 01

Throughline started after one too many engagements that ended in a beautiful PDF and zero shipped product.

We've spent careers inside product and marketing teams, building things, launching things, fixing things that other consultants left behind. We saw the same pattern over and over: smart strategy, polished decks, and then nothing actually changes.

So we built the thing we wished existed. A small, senior team that does the customer work, writes the strategy, *and* helps you execute it. No junior offshoring. No 80-page reports nobody reads. No quarterly steering committees.

Just the throughline, from what your customers actually need to what you actually ship.

[ What We Believe ]

§ 02

Six things we'll
die on the hill for.

01

Customer reality beats internal opinion.

Every roadmap argument we've ever seen could be settled by ten customer conversations. We start there. Every time.

02

Strategy without execution is fan fiction.

A strategy doc you can't act on is just an essay. We deliver work that has a Monday morning version: what to do, who does it, what gets killed.

03

Senior people, doing the work.

No bait-and-switch where partners pitch and analysts deliver. The people in the room are the people in the work.

04

Fixed scope. Fixed price. Fewer surprises.

Hourly billing rewards slowness. We scope tightly, price up front, and finish what we start.

05

Plain language. Always.

If we can't explain it without "synergy," "leverage," or "enablement," we don't understand it yet. Jargon is where bad thinking hides.

06

If it's not the right fit, we'll say so.

Bad engagements help nobody. We'd rather refer you out than waste three months of your runway. Yes, this has cost us deals.

[ Things We Don't Do ]

§ 03

A short list of things you won't get from us.

No

90-slide strategy decks nobody reads past slide 12.

No

Frameworks named after the consultancy that made them up.

No

Year-long retainers designed to never finish.

No

"Our methodology." We use whatever works for the problem.

No

Generic personas built from a focus group of three.

No

Promising things we can't deliver to win the deal.

[ Who You're Working With ]

§ 04

Throughline is led by a small team with deep operating experience, not career consultants.

We've built and shipped product at startups and at companies you've heard of. We've launched brands, rewritten positioning, run go-to-market for new categories, and sat in enough painful customer interviews to last a lifetime. That's the experience we bring to your engagement, not a methodology a McKinsey alum invented in 2008.

For specialty work like design, motion, video, and performance media, we partner with a vetted network of independents we've worked with for years. You get the right person for the job, without paying for an agency layer that doesn't add value.
